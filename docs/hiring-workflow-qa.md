# Hiring workflow release checklist

Use two clean browser profiles: one company account and one new officer account. Do not reuse an officer whose application or offer has already been submitted.

## Candidate and application

- Open a public job link while signed out.
- Confirm name, email, and phone are captured before account creation.
- Create the officer login and confirm the same job application resumes.
- Submit the hiring application and confirm the company sees exactly one immutable employer copy with phone, email, resume, photos, and certificates.
- Confirm both the officer and company receive the application-submitted email.

## Interview

- From Applicants, schedule an online interview and confirm the officer sees the request and receives email.
- Accept the request as the officer; confirm the company sees Accepted and receives email.
- Add the accepted interview to the officer calendar.
- Change the date/link; confirm the officer response resets to pending and an updated email is sent.
- Decline the revised request; confirm the company sees Declined and receives email.
- Reschedule once more, then test both Mark complete and Cancel interview.

## Offer and onboarding

- Mark the interview complete and send an offer.
- Confirm the offer appears once, its PDF opens, and the officer receives email.
- Accept the offer as the officer; confirm the company receives the response and onboarding unlocks.
- Complete and submit every onboarding section; confirm the officer stays in Awaiting company review rather than Hired.
- Confirm the company can preview every onboarding PDF page and download the packet.

## Screening and final hire

- Open Review screening on the applicant card.
- Confirm required checks match the offer terms.
- Leave one required item In progress and verify Accept as hired remains disabled.
- Mark every required check Cleared and save.
- Select Accept as hired and confirm the decision popup.
- Confirm the officer moves from Applicants to Hired only after that confirmation.
- Confirm the officer dashboard and email both say employment was confirmed by the company.

## Failure and audit checks

- Refresh during every pending action and confirm no duplicate application, interview, offer, hire, or notification is created.
- Verify a normal officer cannot read another officer's application, interview, onboarding packet, or screening notes.
- Verify a company team member without hiring access cannot update screening or confirm a hire.
- Verify direct database/client updates cannot confirm employment before onboarding submission and clearance of every required screening item.
- Confirm email retry failures remain queued and do not block the underlying application, interview, offer, onboarding, or final-hire transaction.
